Today we released **TiRex-2**, a multivariate time series foundation model based on xLSTM technology.

We didn't just optimize TiRex -- we added new features! In addition to well-known capabilities such as state tracking and in-context learning, TiRex-2 can handle multivariate time series by processing covariates and enables data streaming without any loss of performance.

At the same time, TiRex-2 places us among the top performers on the most important leaderboards and benchmarks: 
With TiRex-2, we can process a continuous data stream without any loss of performance. This enables entirely new industrial applications using time series.

Learn more about TiRex-2 in [our paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01204), try it out the demo on [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/spaces/NX-AI/TiRex-2-demo), or directly use it in your own [code](https://github.com/NX-AI/tirex-2).
